Papers

Large Teams Overshadow Individual Recognition

2025

As science becomes increasingly team-based, our large-scale analysis of 1.6 million papers reveals that teams amplify the Matthew Effect by obscuring individual contributions, highlighting the urgent need for better credit assignment in collaborative research.

Writing Patterns Reveal a Hidden Division of Labor
in Scientific Teams

2025

Analyzing author-specific LaTeX macros from over 730,000 arXiv papers, we reveal a hidden division of labor in scientific writing—first authors focus on technical sections, last authors on conceptual ones—offering a scalable, behavior-based method to improve credit allocation in team science.
